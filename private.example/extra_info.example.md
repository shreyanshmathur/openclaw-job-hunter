# Extra information

Copy this file to private/extra_info.md (onboarding does it for you) and write in your own words anything
your resume leaves out. The agent treats this file like your resume: it may quote facts from it, but it
never adds anything that is not written here. Numbers you write here are the only numbers it may use
beyond the ones in your resume, so write them exactly.

Delete the example lines (they describe a fictional person) and the sections you do not need.

## Projects and achievements with numbers

What you did, what changed, and how you know. One item per line.

* Example: Built a delivery time estimator in Python that predicts delivery days from pincode pairs.
* Example: Cut the weekly reporting time for the operations team from 8 hours to 2 by automating the carrier
  scorecard.

## Things you want the agent to know

Preferences, industries you care about, the kind of team you want, anything a recruiter should hear first.
